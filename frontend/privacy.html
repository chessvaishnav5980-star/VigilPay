Privacy Policy

VigilPay, last updated 20 November 2026

VigilPay is a free website that helps people spot UPI and online payment scams. It is made by students of Delhi Public School - Mahendra Hills. This page explains, in plain words, what we keep and what we never ask for.

What we never ask for

VigilPay never asks for your UPI PIN, OTP, card number, CVV, bank account number or password for any bank or payment app. If any page or message that looks like VigilPay asks for these, do not enter them.

What we keep if you sign in

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, which Google or your email sign-up gives us.
  • Your quiz scores and game scores, so you can see your progress and appear on the leaderboard.
  • A short history of the checks you ran while signed in, so you can see your recent results.

You can use most of VigilPay without signing in.

What we do with messages, links and numbers you check

Text you paste is analysed to show warning signs. Links may be checked with security services such as Google Safe Browsing and VirusTotal, and numbers may be looked up with phone-data services, so those services receive the link or number you asked about. If you report a number or link to the community, we store only a one-way scrambled version of it plus a category, not the plain value, and not your name.

Language and AI help

Some text is explained or translated with an AI language model. Only the text needed for that request is sent. Translations may contain mistakes, so when something looks odd, trust the English text.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show advertisements or share your details with advertisers.
  • We do not collect other people's phone or bank details.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase. The website runs on Render. Small settings, such as your language and checklist ticks, stay in your own browser.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. You can ask for your account and saved scores to be deleted by contacting the VigilPay student team through Delhi Public School - Mahendra Hills. We will remove them.

Children

VigilPay is made by school students for everyone, including young people. We collect only what is listed above.

If you lose money to a scam

Call 1930 at once and report at cybercrime.gov.in.
